# Raseed V2 Product Vision

## Product definition

Raseed turns everyday receipts into persistent, structured purchase memory. It automatically understands what was bought, stores that information securely, and lets users retrieve, analyze, and act on their financial and household purchase history through natural-language assistance and connected services.

## Core loop

**Capture → Understand → Remember → Reason → Act**

## Core pillars

- Receipt intelligence
- Persistent purchase/financial memory
- Inventory awareness
- Natural-language financial assistance
- Analytics and insights
- Connected actions such as Google Wallet

## Epistemic states

Every material fact should be classified as one of:

- **OBSERVED** — directly present in a receipt or explicitly supplied by the user.
- **DERIVED** — calculated deterministically from stored data.
- **INFERRED** — estimated or reasoned about by a model.
- **EXTERNAL** — obtained from an external service or current market data.

## Product boundary

Raseed is not just an OCR receipt scanner and not just an expense tracker. The receipt is the input mechanism; persistent purchase memory and grounded reasoning are the core product.
